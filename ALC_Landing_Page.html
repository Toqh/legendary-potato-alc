About ALC

Locally led research with a continental horizon.

ALC was created through a joint programme of Wellcome and the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, managed by the Science for Africa Foundation. It supports collaborative research led by Africa-based scientists and strengthens the next generation of research and public-health leaders.

Under the direction of Prof. Ikram Guizani, the consortium builds scientific and public-health excellence for the control and elimination of leishmaniases. Its pan-African scope connects Francophone and Anglophone partners in North, West and East Africa.

The case for action

A preventable burden demands a connected response.

Leishmaniases persist where poverty, limited infrastructure and gaps in access to diagnosis and treatment overlap. Progress depends on linking discovery with delivery.

Scientific objectives

From new technology to stronger policy.

A connected research agenda turns evidence into tools that control disease and improve public-health decisions.

  1. Advance science and technology

    Apply developments in genomics and artificial intelligence to create new tools and strategies for monitoring and controlling leishmaniases.

  2. Improve diagnosis and management

    Use new tools to strengthen disease diagnosis, patient management and vector-control programmes.

  3. Map disease and predict risk

    Build detailed maps of disease distribution and identify risk areas to guide planning and implementation.

  4. Translate research into policy

    Use strategic research and a dedicated Think Tank to inform effective policies and strategies for disease control and elimination.
